## Overall Impression

This is a real product built by someone with taste, undermined by the gap between what it says and what it does. The status vocabulary is genuinely good. The three-doors auth separation is executed properly. Touch and overflow discipline hold up at 390px.

But the product's stated adversary is doubt about counterfeit medicine, and the single biggest opportunity is that **checkout — the moment money changes hands for drugs the customer cannot inspect — contains zero words establishing that Peaceway is a licensed pharmacy.** Grep-verified: no match for licensed, pharmacist, NAFDAC, genuine or verified in `checkout/page.tsx` or `cart/page.tsx`. The landing spends 121 rendered frames arguing the building is real. Checkout spends nothing.

## What's Working

1. **The status vocabulary** (`ui.tsx:12-25`, `347-370`). 21 backend enums mapped to calm human phrases. `failed` becomes "Issue under review" — a deliberate refusal to alarm a sick person. This is the "competent pharmacist" voice, implemented.
2. **Three doors, properly locked.** `/admin` and `/partners` render sign-in gates that actively redirect the wrong audience ("Wholesalers and suppliers should not sign in here"). Cross-domain misrouting is designed against, not just documented.
3. **Mobile fundamentals.** Zero horizontal overflow at 390 and 1280, verified across 9 routes. Zero clipped elements. Zero console errors. `hoverOnlyWhenSupported` is on, so hover never sticks on touch.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Checkout places a delivery order with no address**
- What: `checkout/page.tsx:53` sends `delivery_address: address || undefined`. `handleSubmit` has no guard; the button disables only while submitting. The low-stakes reminder wizard, meanwhile, gates every step.
- Why it matters: A medicine delivery with no address is a failed order, a refund, a staff call, and a customer who concludes the pharmacy isn't real. Validation rigor is inverted — present on the free feature, absent on the money path.
- Fix: Require `address.trim()` before enabling Place Order; inline error on blur. Persist last-used address on the profile and preselect it — this also fixes Flexibility and the refill metric.
- Caveat: source-only. Assessment B could not reach `/checkout` (auth-gated), so this is unverified in a browser.
- Command: `/impeccable harden checkout`

**[P1] No trust evidence at cart or checkout**
- What: Zero matches for licensed/pharmacist/NAFDAC/genuine in checkout or cart. The only nearby copy is `checkout:186` — "By placing this order you agree to our terms of service" at 11px, `text-white/25`, ~2.2:1. Legally-flavoured fine print rendered functionally invisible.
- Why it matters: DESIGN.md says every surface "either reads as evidence that a licensed pharmacy exists behind it, or it reads as another reseller." Checkout currently reads as another reseller.
- Fix: Replace the terms whisper with a legible reassurance block above Place Order: premises address, pharmacist on duty, what happens next. Specificity, per the brand's own thesis — not a badge.
- Command: `/impeccable clarify checkout`

**[P1] The secondary text layer fails the project's own non-negotiable AA standard**
- What: Both assessments converged here. Measured: `text-white/40` = 3.79:1, `/35` = 3.16:1, `/30` = 2.61:1, `/25` ~2.2:1. In use across 146 instances. The bottom-nav labels themselves are 10px at 3.78:1. The address line — the one string proving the pharmacy is physical — renders at 3.15:1 on every route. DESIGN.md defines Ink Muted `#b1bdb0` (~7:1) for exactly this role and it is used once in the entire app.
- Why it matters: PRODUCT.md calls AA "already the committed standard rather than an aspiration"; DESIGN.md calls legibility "a safety property." On screens showing drug names and dosages, on a cheap Android in Lagos daylight, that safety property is failing.
- Fix: Replace `text-white/{45,40,35,30,25}` with two tokens: `#b1bdb0` secondary, `#dcdddb` primary. Raise the 48x 10px and 2x 9px labels to the stated 11px floor. Raise the five 14px form inputs to 16px (iOS zoom).
- Command: `/impeccable audit accessibility`

**[P2] Component vocabulary has fragmented into four button systems**
- What: `.pw-btn` (16px radius), `Button` (`ui.tsx:323`, 12px), `TactileButton`, plus 20 hand-rolled `bg-emerald-500` buttons across admin, start, reminders/new, cart, shop, profile, referral, request, prescription. Radii in the wild: 97x rounded-xl, 87x rounded-2xl, 43x rounded-full, 18x rounded-lg. DESIGN.md specifies one app button.
- Why it matters: The product register's named ban — "if the save button looks different in two places, one is wrong." It's the difference between a licensed operation and something assembled.
- Fix: Delete `Button` and the inline variants; route through `TactileButton`/`.pw-btn`. ESLint rule banning `bg-emerald-500` on `<button>`.
- Command: `/impeccable polish components`

**[P2] Two greens ship side by side**
- What: `drug-icons.tsx:6` hardcodes `const G = "#10b981"` — stock Tailwind emerald-500 — so the entire custom icon family and the pharmacy cross on `/start` are drawn in a different green from the brand `#34d98a` beside them. 15 places. The detector independently caught `rgba(16,185,129,0.12)` at `referral:76`. Also `ui.tsx:61`'s hover glow.
- Why it matters: DESIGN.md's One Light Rule, broken by leftovers rather than by taste. The palette was harmonised in config; the raw hex was left behind.
- Fix: Replace every `#10b981`/`rgb(16,185,129)` with `#34d98a`.
- Command: `/impeccable polish drug-icons`

## Persona Red Flags

**Mrs. Adeyemi (project-specific: Igando customer on mobile data who fears fake medicine)**
- Buying drugs she cannot inspect, from a site found via Telegram. At `/checkout` there is not one word establishing Peaceway is licensed. Grep-verified empty.
- The one line proving the pharmacy is physical — "Peaceway Pharmacy, Igando/Agodo Ikotun, Lagos" — renders at 3.15:1. The central argument is the least legible text on the page.
- `start/page.tsx:26`: the pharmacy cross on her first screen, the mark meant to signal "real dispensary," is stroked in stock Tailwind green.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**
- All 16 routes share one `<title>`. Returning to a 6-tab phone browser, she cannot tell cart from order from shop.
- `checkout/page.tsx:30`: address is `useState("")` with no persistence. Interrupted mid-checkout, she retypes her Lagos address. Every order.
- Bottom-nav labels: 10px at 3.78:1, one-handed, outdoors. She taps by icon position, not by reading.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**
- `checkout:116-142`: `role="radiogroup"` wrapping `role="radio"` buttons with no roving tabIndex and no arrow-key handler. Screen reader announces "radio"; arrow keys do nothing. A native `<input type="radio">` would have worked — the register's "reinventing standard affordances" ban.
- `ui.tsx` contains zero `focus-visible` declarations: `PageHeader`'s back button, `Button` and `MedCard` fall back to the UA ring instead of the specified 3px Signal Green ring.
- Footer nav links are 20px tall with zero padding, on every route.

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**
- Lands signed-out on `/app` and is offered "Staff sign in" and "Partner portal" as options 2 and 3. She is neither.
- "Check Availability" → "My Requests" → "Converted to order", but also "Shop" → "My Orders". Two parallel purchase concepts, both in the nav, never explained.
- Every failure says "Something went wrong. Please try again." (x6). She cannot tell whether her order went through.

## Minor Observations

- `/shop` swallows backend outages: `shop/page.tsx:40-41` catches the fetch rejection and renders "No medicines found" rather than an error. A dead backend looks like an empty catalogue.
- DESIGN.md's own `button-primary` spec (#ffffff on #1a8a50) computes to 4.38:1 — it fails the AA standard the same document calls non-negotiable. The shipped `.pw-btn` (~4.79:1) is more correct than the spec. Fix the doc.
- `tailwind.config.ts` sets emerald-500 to #1aa35a; DESIGN.md says Dispensary Green Lit is #1a8a50. Pick one.
- The accent palette in practice is six colours (signal green, stock emerald, Telegram blue, sky, amber, red). DESIGN.md documents two. Amber/sky for status are defensible — they're just undocumented, so the doc is now lying.
- `PageHeader`'s back button is 36px, under the 44px minimum; `BackBar`'s is 44px and rings correctly. Two near-duplicate components with the same fallback logic.
- Motion discipline is genuinely good: framer-motion is confined to two components, both calling `useReducedMotion()` correctly.
- Coverage gap: `/checkout`, `/admin` and `/partners` dashboards were never browser-audited (auth gates). `admin/page.tsx` carries 36 of the 159 detector findings with zero browser coverage.

## Questions to Consider

1. Your success metric is refills — so why does the app have no memory? No saved address, no reorder, no "order this again". The reminder system knows exactly what Mrs. Adeyemi takes and when she runs out, and it cannot offer to refill it. What if a reminder's end state were a one-tap reorder?
2. If trust is the product, why is it decoration everywhere except where it isn't? 121 frames arguing the building is real; zero words at checkout. Which does a scared customer actually need?
3. Does the customer home need to be the staff lobby? Two of three doors on `/app` serve maybe a dozen people. What if staff and partner entrances lived in the footer, and the space went to the licence, the address, and the pharmacist's name?
