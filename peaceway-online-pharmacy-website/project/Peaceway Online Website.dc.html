Section 06  ·  Ask a Pharmacist

Need help before you buy?

Our pharmacist is available through the Telegram bot. Ask questions about medicines, side effects, interactions, or dosage — before you order.

Prescription-only products require pharmacist review before supply. This is not optional — it is how we keep you safe.

Ask a Pharmacist
Is it safe to take paracetamol and ibuprofen together?
Peaceway Pharmacist
Yes, both can be taken together — but space them 4 to 6 hours apart to reduce the risk of stomach irritation. Avoid taking them at the same time.
I need to refill my blood pressure medication. Do I need a prescription?
Peaceway Pharmacist
Yes, antihypertensive medicines require a prescription. Send your prescription through the bot and I will guide you through the next steps.
