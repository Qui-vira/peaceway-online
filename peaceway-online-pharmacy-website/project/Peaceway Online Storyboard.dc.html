Component Architecture
Frontend structure (Next.js · TypeScript)
One component per section. Shared primitives for repeated patterns. No Telegram API code in Phase 1.
pages/index.tsx ├── <Navbar> — sticky, scroll-aware, logo + 4 links + CTA button ├── <HeroSection> — S1: video BG, logo, headline, 2 CTA placeholders │ ├── <VideoBackground src="seedance_s1.mp4"> │ ├── <HeroHeading> — gradient Syne 800 │ └── <CTAGroup> — Order on Telegram · Join Community (placeholder hrefs) ├── <ProblemSection> — S2: video BG, heading, 3 ProblemCard │ └── <ProblemCard × 3> — icon, title, description ├── <ServicesSection> — S3: video BG, heading, 6 ServiceCard (glass variant) │ └── <ServiceCard × 6> — icon, title, body ├── <TrustSection> — S4: video BG, building photo, 5 TrustPoint │ ├── <PharmacyPhoto> — Ken Burns on scroll │ └── <TrustPoint × 5> — dot, text (no invented details) ├── <OrderingSection> — S5: video BG, 10-step timeline │ └── <TimelineStep × 10> — number, text, connector line ├── <PharmacistSection> — S6: video BG, chat demo, CTA │ └── <ChatBubble × 3> — user + pharmacist alternating ├── <DeliverySection> — S7: video BG, Lagos map SVG, 11 AreaBadge │ └── <AreaBadge × 11> — name, ping animation ├── <CommunitySection> — S8: video BG, 2 TelegramCTA │ └── <TelegramCTA × 2> — Channel + Group (placeholder href) ├── <ContactSection> — S9: video BG, ContactCard │ └── <ContactRow × 4> — Email, Telegram, Address, WhatsApp └── <Footer> — Logo, 4-col links, PCN placeholder, legal text, disclaimer components/primitives/ ├── <VideoBackground> — muted autoplay, IntersectionObserver pause, fallback gradient ├── <SectionHeading> — Syne 800, optional gradient, optional tag label ├── <GlassCard> — backdrop-blur, rgba border, hover glow ├── <SolidCard> — #191A17 bg, border, no blur ├── <FadeUp> — Framer Motion wrapper: scroll-triggered fade + y └── <StaggerGroup> — Framer Motion stagger container
